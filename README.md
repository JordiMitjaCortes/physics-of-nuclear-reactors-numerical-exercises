# Physics of Nuclear Reactors — Numerical Exercises

Numerical exercises completed as part of the **Physics of Nuclear Reactors** course at EPFL.

This repository contains five computational exercises covering neutron diffusion, reactor criticality, multi-group reactor physics, core loading optimization, and isotopic depletion. Each exercise includes a Jupyter Notebook with the numerical calculations and a corresponding technical report.

## Exercises

### Exercise 1 — Neutron diffusion and finite differences
Analytical and numerical solution of a one-dimensional mono-energetic neutron diffusion problem, including finite-difference discretization, boundary conditions, and mesh-size error analysis.

### Exercise 2 — Bare and reflected reactors
Numerical and analytical solutions of the mono-energetic diffusion eigenvalue problem for bare and reflected slab reactors, including effective multiplication factor, neutron flux, and boundary current calculations.

### Exercise 3 — Two-group neutron diffusion
Finite-difference solution of the two-group neutron diffusion k-eigenvalue problem for bare and reflected reactors, including fast and thermal neutron fluxes and higher spatial harmonics.

### Exercise 4 — Core loading and power distribution
Analysis and optimization of reactor loading patterns using a two-group diffusion model, including effective multiplication factor, fast and thermal neutron fluxes, power distribution, peak power, and vessel fast flux.

### Exercise 5 — Isotopic depletion
Analytical and numerical modelling of isotopic evolution under neutron irradiation, including U-235, U-238, Pu-239 and fission-product concentrations, with comparison of numerical integration methods.
